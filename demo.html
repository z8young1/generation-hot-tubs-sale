Good questions

Do I need a Factory Pass to come?
No. The sale is open to the public. The pass gets you the price sheet a day early, first call on floor models and a head start on financing. It is free.
How does the financing work?
Financing is through GreenSky, subject to credit approval. The sale plan is a 12-month promotion: interest accrues from the date of purchase and is waived only if you pay the full amount within 12 months. If you do not, that interest is charged. Full terms are in the GreenSky box above. Apply online before the sale, or at the financing desk during the event.
What is the warranty?
Ten years on the shell structure, three years on the acrylic surface, plumbing and electrical equipment, and one year on the cabinet and auxiliary components. Full terms are at generationhottubs.com/warranty.
Can I get the sale price before November 5?
Sale pricing is only honored at the event, November 5 through 7. Pass holders see the prices a day early so they can plan, but the buying happens at the factory.
What about delivery?
Delivery and placement are scheduled when you buy. Bring a photo of where the tub will go and the team will tell you what it takes to get it there.
