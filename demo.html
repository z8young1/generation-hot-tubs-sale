Good questions

How do I get my factory price?
Fill out the short form on this page and a team member will call you with factory pricing. You can also call 931-414-2908, Monday through Thursday, 7 to 5.
How long is Factory Sale pricing good for?
Factory Sale pricing is honored on orders placed through Saturday, Dec 5.
Can I get it by Christmas?
Yes. Order by Dec 5 and we'll schedule delivery before Christmas.
Can I come Friday or Saturday?
Yes, by appointment. Book a time and someone will meet you.
How does the financing work?
Financing is through GreenSky, subject to credit approval. The sale plan is a 12-month promotion: interest accrues from the date of purchase and is waived only if you pay the full amount within 12 months. If you do not, that interest is charged. Full terms are in the GreenSky box above. Apply online any time, or with the team when you visit.
What is the warranty?
Ten years on the shell structure, three years on the acrylic surface, plumbing and electrical equipment, and one year on the cabinet and auxiliary components. Full terms are at generationhottubs.com/warranty.
What about delivery?
Delivery and placement are scheduled when you buy. Bring a photo of where the tub will go and the team will tell you what it takes to get it there.
